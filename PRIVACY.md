# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-17

Numina Calculator is an offline application. It does not request Internet access and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, user accounts or remote services.

## Data processed on the device

The app stores calculator preferences, memory state and up to 50 recent calculations locally on the user's device. Calculation history is not included in Android cloud/device-transfer backup. Non-sensitive preferences may be backed up by Android according to the user's operating-system settings.

The app accesses the clipboard only when the user explicitly copies a result. Clipboard handling is then controlled by Android and any other software installed on the device.

## Data collection and sharing

Numina does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. No data leaves the device through the app because the application has no network permission or network code.

## Data deletion

Users can clear calculation history inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, subject to Android's own backup settings for non-sensitive preferences.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children or adults.

## Changes

If a future version adds networking, analytics, accounts or any new data processing, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be updated before release.
